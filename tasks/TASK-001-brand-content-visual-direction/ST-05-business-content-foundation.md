## Prohibited or conditional claims

- No customer, supplier, partner, client, or logo names without separate written approval.
- No certifications, awards, customer counts, tonnage, warehouse capacity, delivery duration, product origin, nutrition figures, bag weights, or stock levels without verified evidence.
- No `نستورد مباشرة من الخارج` claim unless direct import operations are later confirmed.
- No `الأفضل في مصر`, `رقم واحد`, or similar superiority claim.
- No “company established for 20 years” wording.
- No English expansion of `زيت منزوع` until the owner confirms its exact commercial name.

## Content voice

### Write like this

- Direct, commercial, and grounded.
- Short sentences that answer a buyer question.
- Clear on product, quantity, availability, customer type, delivery location, and price inquiry.
- Confident without excess promotional language.

### Do not write like this

- Generic corporate claims: `الجودة والتميز والابتكار`.
- AI-template language: `حلول متكاملة لتحقيق أهدافك`.
- Defensive internal notes or compliance statements.
- Long formal introductions before explaining supply and contact.
- Consumer-retail or veterinary-clinic language.
