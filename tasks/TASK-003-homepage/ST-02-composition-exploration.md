# ST-02 — Homepage Composition Exploration

**Status:** Complete — 2026-08-27

## Tool availability

OpenDesign was not available in this workspace. A focused composition review was therefore completed from the approved `DESIGN.md`, TASK-001 homepage architecture, and the temporary repository imagery. No business or product assumption was introduced.

## Adopted ideas

- Full-width operational image as the Hero environment, with copy set inside the page container.
- Directional green overlay that keeps the loading operation visible while protecting Arabic text contrast.
- A compact proof band immediately after the Hero.
- Four-card product preview: soybean-hull family, fava-bean hulls, oil product, and finished-feed family.
- Alternating calm surfaces and one dark audience section to create a continuous commercial journey.
- A simple circular delivery marker instead of a decorative or inaccurate Egypt map.
- An ordered process only where the sequence is meaningful.

## Rejected ideas

- Separate image-card Hero.
- Large repeated category panels and decorative icon treatments.
- Numbered proof, benefits, and audience cards.
- A CSS-drawn Egypt silhouette.
- Extra Hero actions, carousels, counters, animated statistics, or new final imagery.
