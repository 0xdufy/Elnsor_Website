## Handoff brief template

For every approved image-generation conversation, provide:

1. Image ID and final filename.
2. Destination page and component.
3. Exact ratio and target dimensions.
4. Subject and composition.
5. Lighting, realism, and colour treatment.
6. Explicit exclusions: text, logos, brands, packaging labels, unrealistic hands/machinery, CGI gloss, and competitor identifiers.
7. Confirmation that the asset is an illustrative sector image—not a photograph of an Al Nesour facility—unless owner-provided photography says otherwise.
